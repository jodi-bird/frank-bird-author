A disturbing crime in Bellingham, a police operation in Germany, evidence recovered by American special forces in Afghanistan and a child's innocent classroom contribution in the United States appear to have nothing in common.

As GCHQ assembles fragments of intelligence from across the world, connections emerge.

Welsh scientist Josie Maddocks and her colleague Gregoire encounter something altogether different in Africa, animals dying and the first scientific evidence of an extraordinary threat. These isolated incidents display the same developing crisis — one involving the world's most fundamental necessity: water.

Josie and Gregoire are hastily moved to Sweden, where a correlation is found with evidence from a poisoning in Helsinki.
With potentially catastrophic consequences and little time remaining, the Swedish team develops a possible treatment and bypasses the testing.

The novel returns to its mysterious opening: a motionless figure lying inside a sealed glass pod, surrounded by hazmat-suited attendants.

Humanity may have found a way to fight back.
